Returns & Refunds

Last updated: October 2026

Try the ramp with your dog, in your car, for 30 days. If it does not work out, we take it back — used or unused — and refund you in full.

The short version

  1. Email support@pawgrippro.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number.
  2. We send you a prepaid return label the same business day. Return shipping is on us.
  3. Pack the ramp (original packaging if you still have it), hand it to the carrier.
  4. Once the warehouse receives it, your refund is issued to your original payment method.

No restocking fee. No questions.

Wrong size?

If the size is not right for your vehicle, tell us before you ship it back — the size guide and the size finder on the product page exist precisely so this does not happen, and we would rather get you the right ramp than process a refund.

Damaged or faulty

If your ramp arrives damaged, or develops a manufacturing defect, that is covered by the 12-month hardware warranty — a separate, longer protection than the 30-day trial. We cover return shipping on warranty claims too.

Use the contact form — our team replies within one business day.
