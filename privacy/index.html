Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what PawGrip Pro collects when you use this website, and what we do with it. It is written to be read, not to hide behind.

What we collect

  • What you send us. If you use the contact form or the founding-tester form, we receive the details you type in (typically name, email, message) so we can reply.
  • Orders. Checkout runs on Shopify. Payment details are entered on Shopify's checkout and never reach our servers; we see what Shopify shares with us to fulfil the order (name, shipping address, items, order total).
  • Basic site data. Our hosting and analytics providers may process standard request data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed.

Analytics and advertising cookies

When configured, this site loads Google Analytics 4, the Google Ads tag and/or the Meta Pixel to measure ad performance. These set cookies and receive the event data described in our analytics setup (page views, add-to-cart, begin-checkout). The tracking IDs are set by the site owner and can change at any time; when they are not set, no such cookies are loaded.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent it. We do not use it for anything beyond running this store and answering you.

Your choices

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@pawgrippro.com and we will action the request within one business day. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, though checkout requires them on Shopify's side.
