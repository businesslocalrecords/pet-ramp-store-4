Hardware Warranty

Last updated: October 2026

Every PawGrip Pro ramp is covered against manufacturing defects for 12 months from delivery — the frame, the walking surface and the hardware. This is in addition to, and separate from, the 30-day money-back guarantee, which needs no reason at all.

What is covered

  • Frame: cracks, bends or joint failures under the published weight rating.
  • Surface: tread separation or delamination from the deck.
  • Hardware: latches, hinges and feet failing in normal use.

What is not covered

  • Normal wear — scuffs, faded tread, cosmetic marks.
  • Damage from misuse: towing, overloading beyond the published rating, or modification.
  • Chewing or scratching by a pet.

How to make a claim

Email support@pawgrippro.com with your order number and a photo or two of the fault. We reply within one business day. If the claim is accepted we ship a replacement or refund the purchase — your choice — and we cover the return shipping.
