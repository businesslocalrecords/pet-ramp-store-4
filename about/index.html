About PawGrip Pro

Last updated: October 2026

PawGrip Pro makes one product: a foldable dog car ramp for large and senior dogs. Selling one thing well is the whole strategy.

Why this exists

Every day, owners of big and aging dogs make the same trade: lift 70 lb into the trunk, or watch the dog jump. We think there should be a better version of that choice — a ramp that is long enough to be gentle, rated for the dog it is sold to, and honest about what it is.

How we work

  • Published specifications only. We list the ramp's real length, folded size and weight rating, and the size finder on the product page computes the slope you will actually get for your vehicle instead of quoting a marketing angle.
  • No invented proof. We would rather show you fewer claims you can verify than more claims you cannot.
  • A trial, not a trap. 30 days to try the ramp in your own car, a prepaid return label, and a 12-month hardware warranty on top.

Talk to us

Real people, one business day away. If a size or a claim on this site is wrong, we want to hear it.
